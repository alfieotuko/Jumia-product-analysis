#Jumia Product Data Analysis
An analysis of 112 products from Jumia, reviewing how price, discounts, ratings and reviews relate with each other. From the workbook raw data is cleaned, enriched and analyzed to produce compelling dashboard.

##1)Overview 
Original_data - Raw,uncleaned data of 115 products with product name, product prices, discounts, reviews and ratings.
Cleaned_data - After cleaning and removing duplicates 112 products remain, added price category, discount amount, discount category and ratings category.
Analysis - A summary calculations of average price, average discount, average ratings, total reviews and most/least expensive products.
Trend Analysis - top and bottom 5 products by rating together with correlation of the relationship questions raised.
Product performance analysis - Showcasing top products by ratings, and reviews
Relationship Trend analysis - Using scatter charts tool to show correlation of discount and reviews, ratings and reviews and pricing and ratings.
Dashboard - Visualization of detailed summary product performance. 

##2)Dataset 
Product - Product name 
Current price - Listed selling price (KES)
Old price - Original price before discounts (KES)
Price category - Category of prices based on if it is low, medium or high
Discounted Amount - Old price - current price 
Discount - Discount in form of % 
Review - Number of customers reviews 
Rating - Average customer rating of products 
Ratings category - Ratings categorized into poor, average and excellent.

##4)Data Analysis 
- Top and bottom 5 products by ratings 
- Top 10 products by ratings, reviews and discount
- Detailed statistics using average and total

##5)Dashboard 
Create pivot tables of price category, ratings category and discount category which using to create slicers vital for dashboard visualization 

##6)Tools 
Microsoft Excel - Formulas, pivot tables, slicers, pivot chats
